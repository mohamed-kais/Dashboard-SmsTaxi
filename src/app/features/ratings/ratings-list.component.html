How ratings work

The API has no "list all drivers with ratings" endpoint — ratings are only readable per driver. Look up a driver below (by taxi phone or taxi/driver ID) to resolve their driver id, then open their rating history on the detail page.

Per-driver detail lives at /ratings/driver/<id>.

About these numbers
  • Average rating — average from the rating history table (average field of the summary).
  • Aggregate rating — the aggregate stored on the taxi (rating field of the summary).
  • Rating count — number of rating history entries.
  • Per-driver rating history is shown on the detail page (/ratings/driver/<id>).
